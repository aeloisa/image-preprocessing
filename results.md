Techniques tested:
- original image
- grayscale + 2x
- grayscale + 3x
- thresholding + 2x
- thresholding + 3x
- contrast adjustment

Results:
Grayscale conversion and 2x upscaling improved the recognition of some text and measurements. Increasing the scale to 3x did not improve the results. Thresholding at both 2x and 3x upscale and the contrast adjustment did not improve all the results, with some text becoming less accurate than in the grayscale and 2x upscale. Grayscale conversion and 2x upscaling will be used as baseline.
